Long (about 300 words)

BTTN is a free pixel-art roguelite clicker you play in the browser, on a desktop or a phone. There is one glowing Button, and a Horde of hundreds that wants it broken. You are the Hand. Click or simply hold, and your strikes call lightning and meteors while a Warden and the companions you rescue on the way hold the line. Version 4.0, “The Siege”, turns it into a run-based game. Each Siege starts from nothing and crosses three acts and six lands that you pick at doors, each door naming its land, its lord and its reward. After every boss, loot bursts out with ▲ and ▼ against what your party wears, and you take a card. After every lord comes a camp to rest, train, recruit and choose the road. Act bosses give relics that bend the rules. At the end lies the Void: the whole Horde from every side for 75 seconds, then the Mad Button itself. When the Button falls, the run is gone. Your gear burns into Embers that rebuild the town, and the Fame you earned buys small, capped upgrades. Deeds unlock new Buttons to play as, classes, cards, lands and relics, so the next run is different rather than merely stronger. Ten Heat levels, a Daily Siege with a public board and Push On after a win are there for people who play it hard. Streamers can let Twitch chat vote on cards, doors and relics, or summon a treasure goblin. Every run has a seed code viewers can replay. The game is one HTML file of {{SIZE}} with every sound synthesized in code. It is free, with no energy and no paywalls. Nothing random is ever sold, and Gems buy only a Continue and looks, never power.

For streamers and creators

Streaming and monetized videos are welcome. All music and sound are generated in code, so there is nothing to get a copyright claim for.

Streamer mode (4.0)

Settings → Streamer mode, then type your Twitch channel. The game only reads your chat, anonymously; it never logs in and never posts.

!1 !2 !3 vote on cards, doors and relics (15-second windows; you can still click).
!goblin sends a treasure goblin (90-second cooldown), !champ calls the land's champion (once a land), !hype starts a short frenzy (3-minute cooldown).

Share a run

Every run shows a code like BTTN-<seed>-<button>-H<heat>. Viewers enter it at setup to replay the same offers, unranked. The Daily Siege gives everyone the same seed each day, with a public board.

Стримерам и авторам

Стримить и монетизировать видео можно и нужно. Вся музыка и звуки создаются в коде, так что жалоб на авторские права не будет.

Режим стримера (4.0)

Настройки → Режим стримера, затем название канала на Twitch. Игра только читает чат, анонимно; она не входит в аккаунт и ничего не пишет.

!1 !2 !3 — голосование за карты, двери и реликвии (окна по 15 секунд; стример всё равно может кликнуть сам).
!goblin — гоблин с сокровищами (раз в 90 секунд), !champ — чемпион земли (раз за землю), !hype — короткое безумие (раз в 3 минуты).

Поделиться забегом

У каждого забега есть код вида BTTN-<сид>-<кнопка>-H<жар>. Зрители вводят его перед стартом и получают те же предложения, вне рейтинга. Ежедневная осада даёт всем один и тот же сид на день и общую таблицу.

Steam and Next Fest

A Steam edition is in preparation: the same game in a desktop window, with Steam achievements for its Deeds and cloud saves. The plan is to show it at Steam Next Fest in February 2027. On Steam, Gems are earned only: there is no store at all.

Fair play

The whole game is free. Nothing random is ever sold. Gems, earned in play, buy one Continue per run and looks; a few Gem packs and looks can also be bought, never power and never unlocks.

Steam и Next Fest

Готовится версия для Steam: та же игра в отдельном окне, с достижениями Steam за Деяния и облачными сохранениями. План — показать её на Steam Next Fest в феврале 2027. В Steam самоцветы только зарабатываются: магазина там нет вовсе.

Честная игра

Вся игра бесплатна. Ничего случайного не продаётся. Самоцветы, заработанные в игре, покупают одно продолжение за забег и облики; несколько наборов самоцветов и облики можно купить, но никогда — силу или открытия.
