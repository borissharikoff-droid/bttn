Questions

Before you press it

Is it really free?

Yes, the whole game. Optional Gems and looks fund development. No energy, no timers on offers, no paid loot boxes.

Do I need to download or sign up?

No. Press Play. It runs in any modern browser, on phone or desktop.

Is my progress saved?

Yes, automatically in this browser. Settings → Export gives you a save code to move it to another device.

How long is it?

Weeks. 15 lands of 3 zones, a town to build, 19 uniques, 8 relics, and runs that make you stronger each time the Button falls. A session is 3–30 minutes.

Can I play idle?

Yes. Hold the Button (or Space) and the party fights on. While you're away, your garrison and Warden keep earning for up to 4 hours.

Is there gambling?

No real-money gambling: no paid loot boxes, nothing random for sale. Gems buy a Continue or a look, never a roll. Rare drops are earned by playing.

На каком языке игра?

Пока на английском: текста мало, всё понятно по картинкам. Русский перевод уже в работе.
