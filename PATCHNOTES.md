# BTTN — Release

## What's in the game

- **The party.** A Warden plus up to three companions (slots at depths 5, 12 and 20) with roles: tank, healer, damage. Everyone can fall, and a broken Button plus a fallen party is a wipe.
- **Bosses that fight back.** Weak points to tap, DOOM to Ward or Smite, ENRAGE when time runs out, affixes from depth 9, and phases. **Torment 0–10** trades a tougher Horde for more gold, XP and loot.
- **The Town is the hub.** Eleven buildings hold every page and grow with gold for bonuses that last through ascension. **EQUIP BEST** at the Forge dresses the whole party.
- **A busy arena.** Sudden events about every minute, chests by the hundred with Looters, invasions, and the **JACKPOT** (about one kill in two million).
- **Lands, zones and stars.** 15 lands of five zones each, each with its own rule and lord and three stars to earn; then corrupted cycles. **Rifts** are timed runs for the endgame, ranked on a ladder you share with friends.
- **Built for holding the Button.** A held Button clicks once a second, Steady Hand takes it up to 10, and nothing counts past 10.
- **Chase the rare.** Relics drop in a white bag from bosses only, the Lucky Spin pays out from kills, and the JACKPOT hits about one kill in two million.

## 3.1 — Overdrive

- **OVERDRIVE** (V, or the lightning button next to the powers).
  - Fighting charges it: time in the field, faster against a boss, plus clicks and kills. It fills in about a minute and a half.
  - When full, the Button goes white and blue for 8 seconds. Every 0.4 seconds it lashes every mob in sight with lightning for six Warden hits and takes a bite out of a boss. Meanwhile the party attacks twice as fast and gold comes in three times over.
  - Left full for 20 seconds, it goes off by itself.
- **The Gambler**, at the Enchanter's. Spend shards on a mystery item for the slot you pick. It is magic or better, up to the best rarity the depth allows, and about 1 time in 25 it's a unique.
- **Seal an affix** at the Enchanter for shards. An Orb of Flux then rerolls only the other affixes.

## 3.0 — The Town Is Home

- **Everything lives in town.** The side panel keeps only Upgrades and Settings. Every other page has a building:
  - Forge, Enchanter and Alchemist.
  - Tavern: party, character and ladder.
  - Barracks: the Garrison.
  - Museum: collection, relics and achievements.
  - Quest board, Observatory and Hatchery.
  - Temple: ascension.
  - Rift Gate.

  The town opens at any time except during a Rift, and the field holds still while you're there.
- **The town grows.**
  - Each building can be built up five times with gold (800 × 20^level). Each level gives a lasting bonus that is kept through ascension, for example party damage, health, crit damage, potion cap, Garrison gold, item value or Essence.
  - Closed buildings stand as scaffolding.
  - The square has a fountain, stalls, a cat, a dog and chickens, and more townsfolk as the town level rises.
- **Party roles you can see.**
  - The tank walks out to the incoming wave and holds a line of mobs on its shield.
  - The healer stays behind the Button and runs to whoever it mends.
  - Damage dealers work the flanks, and melee companions lunge at their targets.
- **Holding the Button** (or Space) clicks once a second from the start; each level of **Steady Hand** adds one, up to 10.
- **18 new perks and 4 evolutions**, for example Kindling, Frostbite, Executioner, Ricochet, Corpse Blast, Glass Cannon vs Fortress, Momentum, Last Stand and Warband, with their own effects on the field. Auto-pick takes a solid card and never one with a downside.
- **Warden ranks.** Every five levels the whole party gains something visible: an extra mob hit per attack, faster attacks, harder crits, more health or more damage. The XP bar names the next rank.
- **A story.** The first fall of each land's lord opens a chapter card: a line of story, what opened, and where the road goes next.
- **Relics.** A white bag from bosses only, never from Rifts: about 1 in 600 bosses, 1 in 200 lords, and more at higher Torment.
  - The drop plays with slow motion, darkness, a spear of light, the bag bursting open and a reveal card.
  - There are eight relics, each with a rule that changes how you play. The Museum keeps the list.
- **Lucky Spin.** Kills fill a slot machine. Tap it (or press R) for gold, chests, Frenzy, XP or orbs; 7-7-7 is the big one. Mobs also drop "?" bubbles for quick boosts, and crits in a row chain for extra damage.
- **New events.**
  - Portal Storm: portals open by the Button; tap them shut.
  - Warlord: a giant marches in with an escort.
  - High Stakes: gold ×3, but bites ×1.6.
- **Incoming packs.** Rare, champion and special packs flash an arrow with their name at the edge they enter from.
- **A tougher Horde.** Bigger mobs take 1.8 times longer to kill, and bites are a little harder.
- **Gentler first steps.** The first companion joins after depth 3 (was 5), bites are halved for the first three minutes, and the first two lords hit a third softer.
- **Fairer losses.**
  - Only a wipe during a boss costs a depth.
  - After a wipe or a break, no boss comes on its own for 25 seconds. After two losses at the same boss, the party farms for 90 seconds before trying again.
  - Each wipe says why it happened and what to do about it.
- **Calmer start.**
  - The field waits for your first press, and bosses don't come on their own before the tutorial's boss step.
  - Nothing runs on behind an open window.
  - A tip tells you when a party seat opens.
- **Phones.**
  - Building windows are full screen.
  - The slot machine moves to the corner.
  - Bar text is bigger, and keyboard letters are hidden.
- **Other changes.**
  - Garrison fighters are renamed (Cutpurse, Bowman, Hedge Mage, Sentinel) so they don't clash with the classes.
  - A held Button keeps a quarter of its clicks while you're away.
  - The Build-all button builds up every building you can afford.
  - **1, 2, 3** pick a level-up card.
  - Shrine, breach and Hoarder clocks keep running through boss fights.
  - Starting a Rift sends a waiting boss away instead of refusing.
  - Relics have bad-luck protection: every boss without one adds 1% to the odds.
  - The Lucky Spin works from step 5 of the tutorial on.

## 2.4 — The Town

- **The Town** (TOWN at the top right, or **T**): Forge, Enchanter, Alchemist, Tavern, quest board, Observatory, Hatchery. The field holds still while you're there; only the Garrison keeps earning. Closed during a boss fight, a Rift, an invasion, a sudden event or while the Button is broken.
- **EQUIP BEST** (Forge and Party tab) puts the strongest gear you own on the whole party: the Warden picks first, it only swaps in what's stronger, and it never gives a unique to a companion.
- **The Forge** shows what an item would change (damage per second, health, crit, power) before you equip, upgrade (+12% main stat per level, up to +20) or scrap it.
- **The Alchemist** brews potions for gold; each one of the same kind costs 45% more, up to its cap.
- **Hold-to-click** is on by default and clicks 10 times a second (up from 8); no click counts past 10 a second.
- **Four new kinds of the Horde:** Warded (land 2; the Hand does only 8% damage to it, 2.5× health), Chargers (land 2), Menders (land 3; heal nearby mobs 12% every 2 seconds), Callers (land 4; call 7 more every 3 seconds). 12% of packs at depth 1, +0.7% a depth, up to 38%.
- **A tougher, faster Horde:** 4 times the health at depth 1, easing to normal by depth 41; bites at least 3.2% of the Button per unit of weight (was 2.5%); a clear bar needs 18 instead of 25, so a land takes about 4 minutes.
- **A wider field:** about 2.4 times the ground on a computer, about 40% more small fry, at least 90 mobs (was 40), up to 1,100 at once.
- **No waiting on easy ground:** a depth you far outclass clears up to three times faster, an already beaten one up to six times.
- **A readable bottom bar:** icons, plain labels and hover tips on every bar.

## 2.3 — Hard Season

- **A new season:** every save, local and cloud, starts over once. Settings, name and ladder identity carry over.
- **The Horde never shrinks** below full strength (it went down to 40% in 2.2). The first lands have 3.5 times the health at depth 1, gone by depth 41.
- **Bites hurt:** at least 2.5% of the Button per unit of weight (was 0.6%), mob damage +22% a depth (was 20%), Button regen 0.6% a second (was 1.2%).
- **Weak points:** a wind-up is broken by tapping the glowing target beside the boss 3–6 times (one more for a lord), or by Smite. Clicking the Button no longer counts.
- **DOOM** (from the second land): the Button takes 85% of its health and each hero 80% (95% and 90% from a lord). Ward blocks it.
- **ENRAGE:** when time runs out the boss stays 10 more seconds (14 for a lord), striking twice as often.
- **Boss affixes** from depth 9 (two from depth 31): Shielded, Vampiric, Hasted, Regenerating, Frenzied. Champions carry affixes from depth 7.
- **Torment 0–10:** one level per land conquered. Each level: Horde +35% health and +20% bites; +35% gold, +30% XP, +15% loot, +12% luck, +12% fame. No effect in Rifts.
- **Rarer gear opens with depth:** rare 3, epic 6, legendary 13, mythic 25, divine 41.
- **Fame starts at depth 15.** Rally is now 5% a try, up to 15%. Ancestral Might +20% Warden damage a level (was +40%), Boss Slayer +20% (was +35%).

## 2.2 — Hold the Line

- **Bosses on new ground** have at least 12 seconds of your damage as health (30 for a lord); a quarter of that on depths already beaten.
- **Boss blows scale with you:** a regular blow takes at least 9% of its target's health (12% from a lord), a Slam 15% of the Button (20% from a lord).
- **Three powers of the Hand:** Smite (Z, 18 s), Ward (X, 26 s, blocks everything for 3.5 seconds), Mend (C, 40 s, heals the Button 35% and every member 40%).
- **Bites have a floor** of 0.6% of the Button per unit of weight. Overkill clears up to twice as fast, down from four times.
- **Every zone looks different:** dawn, midday, dusk, night and the lord's red ground, each with its own light, weather and scenery.
- **A quieter screen:** fewer loot labels and gold numbers, no Warden name plate, a smaller goal box, and crisp icons on 125% and 150% displays.

## 2.1 — Loot Storm

- **Sudden events** every 45–95 seconds: Stampede, Gold Fever, Chest Rain, Treasure Goblins, Meteor Storm, Crimson Moon, Ambush, the Flood, Adrenaline.
- **Chests by the hundred:** the Horde drops little chests (a tenth of a real one), and the Treasure Hall makes room for 10, 20, 30, 50, 100, then 200.
- **Looters** replace the Key Golem: one chest every 3 seconds (was 8), 10% faster a level; Loot Crew hires up to five. New perk **Plunder**.
- **The JACKPOT:** about one kill in two million, or one real chest in eighty thousand, with odds that climb every hour. It pays 3,000 seconds of income, 24 pieces of gear, a unique, eight orbs and 30 seconds of Jackpot Frenzy.
- **A thicker Horde:** 1.4 times the small fry, up to 900 mobs on screen (was 600).
- **You feel every hit:** red damage numbers, red screen edges, a heartbeat while the Button is under 30%, health bars that leave a trail.
- Five new achievements and many fixes to 2.0 (boss phases, wipes, invasions, the Party tab).

## 2.0 — The Party

- **A party of four:** beat depths 5, 12 and 20 to open a slot each. The Party tab replaces the Character tab.
- **Roles:** knight tank (takes 30% less), cleric healer (new class, heals every 1.4 seconds), archer, wizard and rogue damage. Companions hit for 40% of a Warden with the same gear (a cleric 20%).
- **Stakes:** a fallen member gets up after 24 seconds (8 with a healer), each tap takes 4 off. A broken Button is out for 12 seconds; if the whole party falls too, you fall back one depth.
- **Boss phases:** lords at 66% and 33%, bosses at 50%, each with new moves; a lord's last phase is its rage.
- **Invasions** from depth 3, about every 7 minutes for 90 seconds: Lunar, Cosmic, the Heavenly Crusade, the Abyssal Tide. The Horde comes 1.8× thicker; the herald drops 10–14 items.
- **Two new lands:** the Moon (depths 66–70, Low Gravity) and the Star Sea (depths 71–75, Supernova). Corrupted lands now start at depth 76.
- **Crunch:** small fry take 2.2× the hits and every hit knocks mobs back.
- **A cleaner screen:** a narrower panel (300–372 px), about a third more ground, and on phones a field of 58% of the screen with a folding panel.

## 1.2.1 — Bosses come to you

- When the clear bar is full, a boss the Warden can take arrives after a 3-second countdown; one it can't take yet comes after 60 seconds (20 s with The Hunt). ⚔ still calls it at once.
- A Warden far too strong for the depth clears it up to 4× faster.

## 1.2 — Ready for friends

- **Fixes:** no more freeze after the tab sits in the background, no crash from spitter globs, a safe cloud-save prompt with a backup, and losing a Rift no longer halves the clear bar.
- **Friends:** an in-game invite guide (by email as Editor, public link off), honest status lines, cheat checks on the ladder, and last-seen times on rows.
- **Pacing:** lords have 3× a boss's health (was 4×); every boss rallies the Warden (+15% a try, +20% on a lord, up to five); Rested gives +40% on the first boss after 4 hours away.
- **Unique bad-luck protection:** the 25th chance in a row is a sure thing.
- **The arena is never empty:** under 20 mobs, the next pack comes at once. Slaughter stars need 12,000 to 26,400 kills.
- **Screens:** all 12 tabs fit, landscape phones work, level-up cards and title cards stay out of the way.

## 1.1 — The Horde

- **A real horde:** up to 600 mobs on screen, about 3× the kills per minute, the same gold and XP per second.
- **Four new kinds of mob:** runners, spitters, bombers and tanks.
- **Zones:** every land is five named zones fought in three waves, the last one its lord's.
- **Five new lands past the Mad Button** (depths 41–65): Sunken Library, Clockwork Foundry, Ember Wastes, Mirror Maze, Sky Citadel, each with a rule, a lord and a unique. 19 uniques in all.
- **Land stars:** three per land (Conquered, Slaughter, Swift), each +2.5% damage and gold. A world map and a Land stars board.
- **Carnage:** kill streaks pay +10% at 100 kills, +20% at 300, +30% at 800, +40% at 2,000.

## 1.0 — Loot & Rifts

- **Loot on the ground** with Path of Exile-style label plates, beams and sounds per tier. One drop in 25 upgrades in mid-air; at most 250 drops between legendaries.
- **Currency:** Whetstone, Orb of Flux, Orb of Ascent, Orb of Grace, Orb of Ruin.
- **14 uniques** with fixed affixes and a rule of their own.
- **Lands with rules:** each of the 8 lands changes how the Horde plays; past the Mad Button they return corrupted, +15% loot per cycle.
- **Events:** the Hoarder, four Shrines and the Breach.
- **Bosses fight back** with telegraphed Slam, Summon and Shield moves.
- **Rifts** open after the first lord: 90 seconds to fill the bar, then the Rift Guardian; a clear opens up to +6 levels at once.
- **Eight ladder boards,** a feed, rival notices, inspect and a brag line, all checked on every client and the server.
